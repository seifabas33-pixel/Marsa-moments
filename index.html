07 — Good to know

Frequently asked

When is the best time to visit Marsa Alam?

The Red Sea is a year-round destination. Water sits around 24–29°C, warmest June–October. Winter (Nov–Feb) is mild, quieter and perfect for desert trips. Dolphins and turtles are seen all year.

Do I need to be able to swim or dive?

Snorkelling trips suit all levels — we provide life jackets and guides stay close. For diving, we offer everything from beginner "Discover Scuba" sessions to certified boat dives at Elphinstone.

Is hotel pickup really included?

Yes — free air-conditioned pickup and drop-off from any hotel in Marsa Alam, Port Ghalib and El Quseir. Staying further out? Just ask and we'll arrange it.

How and when do I pay?

Nothing upfront to enquire. Once your plan is confirmed you can pay cash on the day or by card/transfer in advance — whatever's easiest. Prices are per person unless noted.

What if the weather turns?

Safety first. If the captain calls off a sea trip we rebook you for another day or refund in full. Free cancellation applies up to 24 hours before any excursion.

Are the trips family- and beginner-friendly?

Absolutely. Many excursions are great for kids, and our guides adapt the pace to your group. Tell us the ages and comfort levels and we'll tailor the day.
