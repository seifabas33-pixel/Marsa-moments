Wadi El Gemal National Park

⏱ Full day 🏷 Desert 🚐 Hotel pickup included

The 'Valley of the Camels' stretches from mountain to sea — a national park of mangrove lagoons, desert plains and Roman-era emerald mines. By 4x4 we explore Wadi and Qulaan's mangrove islands, watch for gazelles and migratory birds, sip tea with Ababda Bedouin, and finish with a snorkel over untouched shallows.
