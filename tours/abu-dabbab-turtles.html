Turtles & Dugong — Abu Dabbab

⏱ Half / full day 🏷 Sea & snorkel 🚐 Hotel pickup included

Abu Dabbab bay is famous for its shallow seagrass beds — the feeding grounds of large green turtles and, if luck is with you, the elusive dugong (sea cow). Entry is straight off the beach, so it suits every level, and guides point out guitarfish, moray eels and the resident turtles. A gentle, magical snorkel just south of Marsa Alam.
